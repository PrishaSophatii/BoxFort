## Full description
Cats love boxes. They do not love sharing them.

Box Fort is a cozy logic puzzle about one simple rule: **every cat needs its own box.** Drag
cardboard boxes into the room, turn them to fit around the furniture, and tuck every
cat in for a nap. Two cats in one box? Hiss! Every box needs exactly one cat.

It starts easy, then the cats get picky:
🎀 Kittens only fit into small boxes
🐈 Big cats need a roomy one
🪴 Plants, books and yarn are in the way

FEATURES
• Hundreds of levels that keep getting trickier, and every one can be solved
• A fresh Daily Puzzle every day. Keep your streak going and share your result
• No timers, no lives, no stress. Play at your own pace
• Hints when you're stuck
• Lovely hand-drawn cats: ginger, tabby, tuxedo-black, siamese, calico and more
• Small download, plays offline

If you like sudoku, nonograms, tangrams or block puzzles, and you like cats, Box Fort is
your new favourite way to relax.
