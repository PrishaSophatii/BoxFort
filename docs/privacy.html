Box Fort – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Box Fort ("the app") is a puzzle game. This policy explains what information is collected when you play it.

Information the app stores on your device

Your game progress (unlocked levels, hints, daily puzzle streak) is saved only on your device. We don't have accounts and we don't receive this information.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from it, device type and how you interact with ads. Google's use of this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button on the home screen. On iOS, the app asks for permission before using the advertising identifier, and you can change this in Settings. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

We don't sell your information. Apart from the advertising described above, no information leaves your device. When you tap "Share result", you choose where your result text goes.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
